About us

Building dreams, brick by brick.

Your trusted source for quality bricks and pavers at competitive prices, for every build from a first home to a multi-storey site.

Who we are

Quality bricks, straight from reliable manufacturers.

Pangano Bricks is a building materials supply business. We connect customers with reliable brick manufacturers and make quality bricks and paving easy to get, without unnecessary complications.

We cater to a wide range of customers: individuals building their dream homes, contractors, property developers, corporate organisations, and companies taking on large residential, commercial and multi-storey projects.

Whether you need bricks for walls or pavers for driveways, walkways, patios and outdoor spaces, we help you find the right product at a competitive price, with dependable service from first message to delivery.

Our mission is to make quality bricks and pavers accessible to every builder, one project at a time.

How it works

From the kiln to your site, in four steps

01

Made

Our manufacturers mould the bricks to a consistent size and dry them in the open air.

02

Fired

They're fired in the kiln. The heat gives the strength and the colour.

03

Matched to your job

Tell us what you're building. We point you to the right brick or paver and quote a competitive price on WhatsApp.

04

Delivered

Your order is cubed, strapped and delivered straight to your site, anywhere.
