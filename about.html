About us

Built on quality, run from the yard.

Pangano Bricks makes and supplies bricks, paving and flooring materials for builders across Zimbabwe, from single-room extensions to full commercial sites.

No middlemen

Straight from where it's made.

No middlemen, no marked-up resellers, just materials straight from where they're made. We keep it simple: message us on WhatsApp with what you need, we quote it straight, and we get it to your site.

Whether it's a single-room extension or a full commercial build, the process is the same yard-direct pricing and the same delivery team.

How an order runs

From yard to site, in three steps

01

Manufacture

Bricks, blocks and paving are manufactured at the yard to the specification your project needs.

02

Cure & Stack

Product is cured, then stacked and strapped onto pallets ready for collection or dispatch.

03

Load & Deliver

Our team loads the order and delivers it straight to your site, subject to location and order size.
