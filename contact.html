FAQs

Common questions

What are your hours?

We're open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Message us on WhatsApp any time.

Which brick should I use?

Common bricks for foundations and any wall you'll plaster; face bricks for walls you want to leave bare; rustic face bricks for pillars, feature walls and braai areas. See the full brick guide, or send us your plan and we'll advise.

Do you deliver to my area?

Yes, we deliver anywhere. Send us your site location on WhatsApp and we'll include the delivery cost in your quote.

Is there a minimum order?

We take custom quantities. Tell us what you need and we'll quote it, including whether delivery or collection makes more sense for the size of the order.

Can I collect from the yard?

Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a collection time and give you directions to the yard.

Can I split deliveries across my build?

Yes. Tell us the phases of your project (foundation, walls, paving) and we can schedule deliveries to match, so bricks aren't sitting on site for months.

How do I pay?

Payment options are confirmed with your quote on WhatsApp. Only pay using the details given by our official number (see below).

Beware of fake suppliers. Pangano Bricks only takes orders and payments through our official WhatsApp number linked on this website. If someone claiming to be Pangano asks you to pay into a different account, don't pay. Message us to confirm first.
