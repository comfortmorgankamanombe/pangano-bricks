Our bricks

Different bricks for different structures and environments

Different bricks serve different purposes depending on their strength, appearance and durability. Here's everything we supply and where each one belongs.

Brick types and where to use them
Brick typeBest used forQuote
Red Common Bricks General house walling, walls you'll plaster inside and out, boundary walls Quote
Load-Bearing Bricks Structural walls that carry the building's weight, multi-storey and commercial builds Confirm spec with your builder Quote
Face Bricks House exteriors and any wall left bare, with no plaster or paint Quote
Red Rustic Face Bricks Exposed and feature walls, pillars and gate posts, braai areas Quote
Black Rustic Face Bricks Modern decorative designs, contemporary exteriors, feature walls Quote
Semi-Common Bricks General walling that will be plastered, extensions, outbuildings Quote
Blue-Heart Bricks General walling and other uses allowed by the product specification Quote
Cement Bricks A wide range of construction uses: walls, boundary walls, outbuildings Quote
