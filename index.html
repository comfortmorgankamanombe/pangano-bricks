Our bricks

The right brick for every part of the build

Every brick is moulded, dried and fired at our own yard. Here's what we make and where each one belongs.

Common Bricks

Our everyday, hard-burnt clay brick. Strong, consistent and affordable: the workhorse for any wall that will be plastered.

Best for

  • Foundations
  • Load-bearing walls
  • Plastered walls
  • Boundary walls

Face Bricks

Clean edges and an even finish, made to be seen. Build it once and leave it bare, with no plaster and no paint to maintain.

Best for

  • House exteriors
  • Unplastered walls
  • Boundary walls
  • Low maintenance

Rustic Face Bricks

A rough, textured face with natural dark-to-red tones from the kiln. No two walls look the same, which gives you character without extra cost.

Best for

  • Pillars & gate posts
  • Feature walls
  • Braai areas
  • Garden walls
