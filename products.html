01 — Bricks

Common Bricks

Hard-burnt clay bricks for the structure of the building: the walls you'll plaster, the foundations you'll bury and everything in between. Consistent size means faster laying and less mortar.

  • Use for: foundations, load-bearing walls, internal and external plastered walls, boundary walls
  • Finish: plain, made to be plastered or painted
  • Supplied: stacked in cubes, by the thousand or any custom quantity

02 — Bricks

Face Bricks

Face bricks are made to be seen. Even colour and clean edges mean the wall is finished the day it's built, with no plaster, no paint and nothing to redo every few years.

  • Use for: house exteriors, unplastered walls, boundary walls, gate surrounds
  • Finish: smooth, even face with natural fired tones
  • Why: saves on plaster and paint, and weathers well in sun and rain
Quote for face bricks
03 — Bricks

Rustic Face Bricks

Rough, textured faces with dark-to-red kiln tones that change from brick to brick. They give a wall instant character, which suits pillars, feature walls and outdoor living spaces.

  • Use for: pillars and gate posts, feature walls, braai areas, garden and boundary walls
  • Finish: textured, multi-tone face, left unplastered
  • Tip: mix with plain face bricks for banding and corners

04 — Paving & tiles

Paving & flooring

Interlocking Pavers

Y-shaped pavers that lock into each other, so the surface spreads the load and stays level. Mix red, charcoal and grey for a pattern, or keep one colour for a calmer look.

  • Use for: walkways, patios, pool surrounds, courtyards
  • Colours: red, charcoal, grey, or a mix
Ask about interlocking pavers

Rectangular Pavers & Edging

Brick-shaped pavers that can be laid herringbone, basket-weave or straight. Herringbone is the strongest pattern for cars turning. Add a contrasting border to frame the drive.

  • Use for: driveways, carports, parking, garden borders and edging
  • Patterns: herringbone, stretcher bond, basket-weave
Ask about driveway pavers

Flooring Tiles

Thick concrete tiles for outdoor floors that take foot traffic, sun and rain. Sold by the unit or by the pallet, depending on what the project needs.

  • Use for: verandas, outdoor floors, garden paths, stoeps
  • Colours: red and grey
